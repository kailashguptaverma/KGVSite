02 / Behind the projects

I like figuring out
how things could work.

It started with trains, cars, and LEGO. Joining my high school robotics team turned that curiosity into a love for designing and building.

Now I’m studying mechanical engineering at Washington University in St. Louis, with minors in robotics and computer science. I’m happiest when I’m moving between CAD, the workbench, and a prototype that needs one more adjustment.

Outside engineering, you’ll usually find me hiking, cooking, or dancing with WashU Bhangra.
